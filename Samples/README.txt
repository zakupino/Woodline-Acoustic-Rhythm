NO AUDIO SAMPLE FILES ARE EMBEDDED IN THIS INITIAL SOURCE BUILD.

The current instrument sound is generated by its plucked-string DSP engine. A proper multisample playback engine and bundled sample content are the next sound-quality phase.

A suitable public-domain source has been identified:
- Meta Open Sound — Meta Steel Guitar
- 375 steel-string samples, mapped string-by-string over E2–C6
- Three separately recorded velocity layers (soft, medium, hard)
- 24-bit / 48 kHz WAV master release, plus OGG preview archive
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (commercial use, modification and redistribution permitted)
- Source: https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound
- Collection specification: https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/tree/main/systems/instruments/meta-steel-guitar
- Release: https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/releases/tag/meta-steel-guitar-v1.0.0

The sample engine is NOT integrated into this build yet. Do not assume that the current VST3 contains these recordings. The next implementation should bundle the sample bank with the VST3, map the three velocity layers, select physically appropriate strings for repeated pitches, and stream/cache samples without blocking the audio thread.
